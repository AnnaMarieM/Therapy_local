1. **Anxiety about checking locks**
   Tomas checks the door and stove thirty times a night. It takes him an hour to leave the apartment.
   > Thirty times a night I check if I locked the door and turned off the stove, and it takes me an hour to leave the apartment.

   > I feel relieved for a moment. And then an image comes to my head that I didn't do it properly, and I have to do it again.

   > Every day. It's worst in the evening when we go out or sleep. In the morning too.

   Tomas has a feeling that he can cause the fire and will be guilty if he doesn't control something.

   > I try to leave it. And then anxiety grows in me until I feel like I'm going crazy. I'd rather do it.

   > Heart, sweat. My hands are trembling and I feel sick to my stomach.
