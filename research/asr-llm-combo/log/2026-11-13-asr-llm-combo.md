# Experiment log: ASR x LLM combo and the final workflow, 2026-11-13

Purpose: one place where an agent can see what was tried on 2026-11-12/13, how it went and what it cost, so that failed work is not repeated. It is a log, not a result set. Nothing here has been through the run ledger (`final-workflow/RUNS.md` does not exist yet).

Read with: [`final-workflow/FINDINGS-2026-11-13.md`](../final-workflow/FINDINGS-2026-11-13.md) (corrections and open items), [`final-workflow/PLAN.md`](../final-workflow/PLAN.md) (workflows, controls, scope cut), [`../README.md`](../README.md) (WER table), `reports/`, `reading-notes/`, `device/README.md`. Condensed agent transcripts: `log/transcript-notes/` (sampled, partly truncated).

Status tags in the last column:
- **VERIFIED**: the number is in a file in the repo (path given). Where I recomputed it, I say so.
- **TRANSCRIPT-ONLY**: seen only in an agent transcript note; no output file in the repo. Treat as a claim, re-measure before relying on it.
- **CONTRADICTED**: a file disagrees with what an agent or FINDINGS said; the row says what.

Conventions. Times are the transcript clock (the notes put AD-18 at "02:45 CET" when the transcript says about 01:43, so the transcript clock is probably UTC and CET is +1; this is an inference). WER is against the script as read, numbers folded to words, diacritics folded (`e2e/combo/common.py`). Recordings: Czech jana-n, tomas-b; English daniel-r, hannah-w (synthetic, AD-20). Devices: Poco = Xiaomi 2510DPC44G (Snapdragon 8 Elite), Pixel = Pixel 8 (Tensor G3), Tab = Galaxy Tab S7 (SM-T970). "Mean" in a WER cell is the mean of the two recordings of that language unless stated. Only four recordings, two per language, tuned against themselves: a gain under about 0.5 point is noise.

## Dead ends and what not to repeat

- **Canary is not the best ASR here.** Whisper large-v3 and turbo beat it on both languages in CI and on the T4. AD-18 ("Canary stays") rested on Whisper small only. Do not rely on AD-18's premise.
- **Slowing the audio (`atempo` 0.5-0.9), denoise, loudness normalisation, beam 10, previous-text conditioning, VAD off, domain prompt:** each moves WER by about 1 point or makes it worse (CI round 2a and 3a, T4 e3). Slowing is monotone worse. Stop tuning decoding options.
- **Greedy plus domain prompt:** bad on English (daniel-r 23.8%, hannah-w 36.2%). Use beam 5 without prompt.
- **Czech fine-tunes (CzechSpeech/Moravian, BuzzASR, mikr cv13):** worse than large-v3 on conversational Czech; Moravian forgets English, Buzz and Moravian loop on characters. mikr is the only near miss and still loses 2-3 points.
- **Word-list constrained decoding:** at most a 0.5-point mean gain, 2-4x slower, and it breaks turbo at penalty 6 and both models at 12. An earlier list built from the scripts is invalid (leaked).
- **Whisper small and medium for Czech:** 42% and 29% WER.
- **Per-turn Whisper on the T4:** costs about 7 WER points on Czech and 4-5 on English (lost context), even with oracle boundaries. Whole-recording transcription followed by speaker assignment is the open alternative.
- **Whisper translate (Czech audio to English text) except with large-v3:** and even there it inverted the self-harm denial in the app clip. The harm clause was lost or reduced in every T4 variant.
- **Gemma E2B listening to Czech audio:** garbles Czech, loses the self-harm sentence, leaks prompt text. E4B is borderline and needs the domain prompt. Do not use E2B as the Czech listener without a fix.
- **E2B for the self-harm item after pivot:** wrong direction in all 4 lanes in CI round 2b.
- **Hexagon NPU for Whisper large-v3:** decode is 4x slower than CPU (42.9 s vs 11.3 s) and the NPU-encoder + CPU-decoder hybrid aborts. NPU wins only for turbo q8_0 (16.5 s vs 20.4 s, and that CPU run was disturbed).
- **Vulkan on Mali and Adreno 650, OpenCL on Adreno 650, Vulkan q5_0 on Adreno 830:** failed or slower than CPU (Pixel Vulkan 88 s vs CPU 35 s). The Tab cannot use the Hexagon NPU (v66).
- **Whisper q5_0 on the NPU:** not supported on HTP, falls back to CPU, no gain.
- **llama.cpp CUDA on the Colab T4:** build is long, hit exit 126, `nvcc` terminated, missing `libcublas.so.12`. Budget for it or use a prebuilt wheel/container instead of building on the session.
- **Running anything heavy in `:batch` without checking thread priority:** background priority (nice 10) slowed Whisper about 8x (claimed) and Pixel Canary runs; earlier on-device timings may be pessimistic. Pixel throttles hard when hot, locked, or on battery (Gemma GPU load 325-344 s).
- **Foreground `sleep` in tool calls and `colab exec` with several arguments:** refused or rejected; use a Monitor/until-loop and a script file.
- **Wikidata SPARQL at scale:** rate-limited (HTTP 429, 1 request per minute).
- **Trusting a scorer you did not read:** the ad hoc app-side diff scorer gave daniel-r 26.4% until leaked special tokens were stripped (19.1%).
