## What
- Onboarding picks one of two workflows; the phone downloads its models in the background. The picker recommends Quality on a flagship (Snapdragon 8 Elite class). Default: Fast.
- Both diarize first and work per turn (AD-06); both process in English and translate only what is shown (AD-17).
- **Fast** (all Gemma 4 E2B): diarize; E2B listens to each turn and returns the Czech transcript and the English translation in one answer; E2B decides the roles; E2B extracts the facts; if the app is in Czech, the facts are translated back.
- **Quality** (Whisper large-v3 + Gemma 4 E4B): diarize; Whisper large-v3 (int8, whisper.cpp) transcribes each turn straight into English, so there is no Czech transcript; E4B decides the roles; E4B extracts the facts; if the app is in Czech, the facts are translated back.
- Canary is no longer the speech recogniser in either workflow, and Whisper turbo is not offered (it cannot translate).

## Why
- Real-voice test, 2026-11-13, four recordings (`research/asr-llm-combo/`, run ledger in `research/asr-llm-combo/final-workflow/RUNS.md`): Czech WER Canary 22-23%, Whisper turbo 16-17%, large-v3 14-15%. AD-18 rested on Whisper small only.
- English facts beat Czech facts (round 2b): E4B coverage 73% vs 68%, lane stability 89% vs 79%; E2B 65% vs 52%.
- In an earlier benchmark large-v3 q8_0 ran on a Poco (8 Elite), Pixel 8 and Tab S7 with no memory kills; a 30 s chunk took 21 / 99 / 114 s. Quality is practical on a flagship only, so it is a choice, not the default; the final test uses the Poco only.
- One model (E2B) for Fast means no second engine to ship.
- Known risks, each with a gate: Whisper's own translation inverted "not that I'd want to" in the self-harm passage and leaves no Czech transcript to check it against (round 1, one passage); Gemma 4 E2B reversed who harms whom in that passage in 4 of 4 lanes in the text-only pivot test; Gemma 4 audio accuracy in Czech and its runtime support in the app are unmeasured.
- Accepted when measured on the final build (`final-workflow/PLAN.md`): (1) Fast keeps every critical item or its failures are listed and accepted; (2) Quality does not invert or drop the self-harm denial; (3) translated-back Czech keeps the meaning; (4) times are known on the Poco (the only test device for this decision); (5) all four recordings complete on the Poco in both workflows.
