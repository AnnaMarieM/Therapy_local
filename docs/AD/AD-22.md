## What
- Onboarding asks two questions; the phone downloads the chosen models in the background.
  - **Transcription:** Fast = Canary 1B v2 (today). Balanced = Whisper large-v3-turbo. Best = Whisper large-v3. Default: Balanced.
  - **Understanding:** Fast = Gemma E2B. Smart = Gemma E4B. Default: Fast.
  - The picker recommends Best on a flagship (Snapdragon 8 Elite class) and Balanced elsewhere.
- Whisper runs through whisper.cpp at int8 (q8_0) on the CPU. An NPU or GPU path may follow with the same weights (AD-21).
- One pipeline for every tier (AD-17): diarize, transcribe each turn in the client's language (AD-06), Gemma translates to English, roles and facts in English, translate back only for display. Whisper's own translate task is not used.

## Why
- Real-voice test, 2026-11-13, four recordings (`research/asr-llm-combo/`). Czech WER: Canary 22-23%, turbo 16-17%, large-v3 14-15%. English: 15%, 9%, 8%. AD-18 rested on Whisper small only.
- On phones, large-v3 q8_0 and turbo q8_0 run on a Poco (8 Elite), Pixel 8 and Tab S7 with no memory kills. A 30 s chunk takes 21 / 99 / 114 s (large-v3) and 20 / 35 / 72 s (turbo). Best is practical on a flagship only, so it is a choice, not the default.
- English facts beat Czech facts: E4B coverage 73% vs 68%, lane stability 89% vs 79%; E2B 65% vs 52%. A test-only prompt that adds "the text is machine-made, skip what makes no sense" lifted E2B to 75%; the app has no such prompt, so that is not claimed here. Whisper translate is usable with large-v3 only (turbo has none), inverted "not that I'd want to" in the self-harm passage and drops the Czech transcript. Transcribe-then-Gemma got the meaning right in 4 of 4 lanes (E4B).
- Accepted risk: E2B reversed who harms whom in that passage in 4 of 4 lanes. E4B did not.
- Not measured yet: the real diarizer with per-turn transcription (the test ran on whole recordings), Canary and turbo transcripts through the English pivot, and the translation back to Czech.
