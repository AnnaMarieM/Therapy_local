## What
- The recordings in `e2e/corpus/human/` (Hannah, Daniel, Jana, Tomáš) are read from scripts written by Claude. They hold no real client or patient data.
- They may be committed to the public repo, uploaded as CI artifacts and run on cloud runners (the ASR × LLM combo test).
- Real session recordings stay out of git and CI (`.gitignore`, `qa/eval-private/`).

## Why
- The cloud test needs the audio on a runner, and the repo is public.
- The scripts are fictional, so there is nothing to protect.
