# AD-18: Canary stays the speech recogniser because of its Czech; Whisper is not added back

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | Accepted |
| Decided | `2026-11-13 02:45 CET` |
| Decided by | Martin Hajný (@hajnina) |
| Drafted by | Claude (Sonnet), branch `feature/english-pivot` |

## What
- Canary 1B v2 remains the only speech recogniser (AD-02). It was picked for its word error rate on Czech, which is what most sessions are in.
- Whisper is not brought back as a second engine or a setting, even though it scored better on English in our test.
- Any future ASR change is judged on Czech first, then English, on the real-voice corpus (`e2e/corpus/human/`).

## Why
- Real-voice test, 2026-11-13, same scoring (word-level, diacritics and punctuation removed, special tokens stripped): Czech: Jana Canary 23.5% against Whisper small 35.1%, Tomáš 36.2% against 43.7%. English went the other way: Daniel 19.1% vs 16.9%, Hannah 19.7% vs 14.7%. `large-v3-turbo` was not run; see #110.
- Czech is the main language, so the Czech result decides. The English gap is tracked in #110 and may be closed by fixing the token leak (#107) and the merged turns (#108), not by a second engine.
- Rejected: Whisper as a per-language engine for English. It brings back the engine setting, per-language downloads and a second set of models that AD-02 removed on purpose.
- Accepted cost: English accuracy is about 3 to 5 points worse than Whisper small on this corpus until #110 is worked.
