# Marta Novak (client B) — shared narrative bible

**Basics:** 29, UX researcher at a health-tech startup (Greenfield Health), Manchester. Moved from Bratislava at 22. Single, living alone in a flat in Chorlton. Cat: Pepa. Therapist: Dr. Anna.
**Presenting problem (intake, Feb):** grief after her mother's death about 7 months before intake (died 19 July last year, pancreatic cancer, diagnosed to death in 11 weeks), insomnia, guilt about not being there at the end.
**Family:** Mother Elena (d. at 58), a seamstress, warm, sang while cooking. Father Pavol (61, in Bratislava, withdrawn, remarried-ish partner Zuzana within months, which Marta resents). Brother Michal (33, stayed in Bratislava, caretook mother, resentful that Marta "left"). Grandmother Babka Ruzena (86).
**Key guilt event:** Marta was in Manchester on a work launch when mother deteriorated; arrived 2 days before death; mother was mostly sedated. Last call a week earlier: Marta cut it short ("I'm busy, I'll call tomorrow").
**Work:** manager Sam (supportive, offers leave), colleague Aisha (friend). Marta over-works to avoid feeling; missed deadlines in spring, then overcompensated. Applied for a bereavement counselling group, never went.
**Health/habits:** sleeps 3-4 h, wakes at 3 a.m. replaying the phone call; stopped cooking (mother's recipes); avoids the song Elena used to sing; cries in the shower; no alcohol problem; skips meals. Panic attack on a tram in session 5.
**Social:** friends Jana (from Slovakia, in London) and Aisha; dating app deleted after a failed date in session 4 where she cried. Ex Tomas (ended 2 yrs ago).
**Hidden/late reveals:** (s5) she has not opened mother's last voicemail; (s7) anger at mother for hiding the symptoms for months; (s8) she took mother's thimble and carries it.
**Arc over 10 sessions (Feb → Oct):**
1 intake, mother's death story. 2 the last phone call guilt. 3 work overload, Sam offers leave. 4 failed date, loneliness, Jana. 5 tram panic attack, the unopened voicemail. 6 father and Zuzana, conflict with Michal. 7 anger at mother for secrecy. 8 trip home for the first anniversary of death (19 July), grave visit, thimble. 9 cooking mother's recipe for the first time (halusky), listens to voicemail. 10 review: sleep improved, still grieving, plans for visiting Babka.
**Therapy style:** grief-focused, person-centred, some CBT for sleep; therapist is warm, uses silences.
