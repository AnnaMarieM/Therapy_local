# George Hale (client A) — shared narrative bible

**Basics:** 41, senior auditor at Brightwell & Co. (mid-size accounting firm), Bristol. Married to Laura (39, primary school teacher) for 9 years. Dog: Milo (beagle, 6). Therapist: Dr. Anna (first-name basis with George).
**Presenting problem (intake, Jan):** long-term burnout, trouble switching off, "I shut down when criticised", marital distance, ambivalence about having children.
**Family:** Father Richard (71, retired engineer, harsh and exacting, never said "well done"; grades of 9/10 were met with "where did the other point go?"). Mother Joan (68, conflict-avoidant, "keeps the peace"). Sister Claire (37, lives in Leeds, two kids, the "easy" child; visits sometimes).
**Work:** Boss Dennis Marsh (52, partner, publicly critical, rewrites George's reports). Colleague and friend Priya (supportive). George was passed over for promotion to Manager in March; a junior (Tom) got it. Works 55-60h weeks, stays late, answers email on weekends.
**Health/habits:** poor sleep (wakes 4 a.m.), drinks 2-3 glasses wine most nights since summer, stopped running (used to run half-marathons), GP prescribed nothing yet; considering it. No suicidal ideation; occasional "what's the point" thoughts, reported honestly in session 6.
**Relationship:** Laura wants a child, George is unsure ("afraid of making the wrong choice, or of becoming my father"). Laura asked "are you unhappy with me?" and he could not answer. They have not had sex in months. A trip to Lisbon is planned for their anniversary (Nov).
**Hidden/late reveals:** (s4) he once cheated on a school exam out of fear of his father's reaction; (s7) he checked a job ad at a rival firm; (s8) his father had a health scare (angina) and George felt nothing, then guilt.
**Arc over 10 sessions (weekly-ish, Jan → early Oct, with some gaps):**
1 intake, burnout, work overview. 2 father's expectations, grades. 3 Laura and the children question. 4 exam-cheating memory, perfectionism. 5 boss Dennis criticism of a report, shutdown. 6 drinking, sleep, "what's the point" thoughts. 7 job ad, Priya, fantasy of leaving. 8 father's health scare, guilt/numbness. 9 honest conversation with Laura, Lisbon planning, boundary at work (refused weekend work once). 10 review: what changed, still unsure on children, goal list.
**Therapy style:** CBT-informed, person-centred; therapist reflects, asks open questions, rarely interprets.
